# The Fate of Conserved Water

Manuscript source (LaTeX) for a framework paper on the fate of conserved water: consumptive vs non-consumptive savings, the disposition of freed water, timing and location effects, a typology of conservation practices, and two-ledger Sankey diagrams.

Build: `latexmk -pdf main.tex` (or open in Overleaf). Regenerate the Sankey figure with `python figures/make_sankey.py`.
